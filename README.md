## About Me

I'm a **Computer Science (AI & ML) undergraduate at JNTUH**, focused on building practical AI/ML applications and reliable software systems. I enjoy working across **machine learning, Generative AI, backend development, and data-driven applications**, turning ideas into products that go beyond a notebook demo.

I've built projects like **RoleRadar**, an AI-powered job-matching platform that combines automated job discovery, resume understanding, semantic matching, and personalized recommendations, and **AutoAnalytx**, an agentic AI data analyst that turns natural-language questions and raw datasets into actionable insights.

Outside of projects, I spend time strengthening my **DSA and core CS fundamentals**, solving problems involving dynamic programming, graphs, and optimization. I enjoy taking a problem from **idea → implementation → deployment**, with a focus on building systems that are useful, reliable, and easy to use.
